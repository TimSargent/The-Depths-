Heavier industrial miner skin, inspired by the original silhouette concept. Toggle silhouette, gear detail, rig or skeletal overlay. All animation poses are preserved.

Side view

Modular canvas skin uses original skeletal joints. Helmet, gloves, boots and backpack are independent render attachments. Closed fists rotate with the handle. Wrist alignment is solved per frame, and torso windup and impact recoil are strengthened. Depth-aware FK drives the standing cross-body, crouched side and rising strikes, and all three prone strikes. Crouched overhead and other standing strikes are preserved. Prone keeps its supporting hand fixed. Prone has no overhead option. No damage or full skin-mesh collision checks are implemented. Side-view silhouette is the primary design; camera views are projected 2.5D artwork.
