Rigged miner test • 4 seconds per strip
Hold RIGHT in Excavate mode. The miner will chip forward through the wall, collect rubble in his duffle bag, and automatically return to the shaft ledge when the bag is full.
Each thin horizontal section takes 4 seconds to mine. Four sections make one full tunnel-length tile. New crawl tunnels need the least excavation; crouch tunnels need twice as much. Standing tunnels require supports. The miner cannot dig downward. Controls: hold left or right (keyboard A/D or arrow keys on a computer). When the bag fills, he automatically returns to the ledge, empties it, and resumes.
